Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the package price?
Every package includes private transportation in an air-conditioned vehicle with a licensed English-speaking chauffeur/guide, handpicked accommodation, daily breakfast, and all government monument entrance tickets listed in the package itinerary.
Can I customize dates, duration, or locations?
Yes, absolutely! All our packages can be modified to match your exact arrival dates, preferred pace, or special interests. Just mention your requests in our contact form or send us a WhatsApp message.
How do payments and deposits work?
Once we finalize your custom plan via email, we take a 20% advance deposit to secure your hotel bookings and private driver. The remaining balance can be settled easily via bank transfer or card prior to arrival.
