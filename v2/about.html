About us

Lanka Travellers is a small team that believes the best way to see Sri Lanka is with someone who calls it home.

Our vision

A Sri Lanka where every visitor leaves with real stories and every community they visit benefits.

Our mission

To plan safe, well-paced and honest tours, using local guides, family stays and fair prices.

Our journey

  • 2015 Founded in Kandy with two cars and one route.
  • 2018 Opened a Colombo office and added the southern coast.
  • 2021 Launched Ella and hill country tours.
  • 2025 Welcomed our 2,500th guest.

Why choose Lanka Travellers?

Local guides

Licensed guides from the regions you visit.

Honest pricing

Every fee is listed before you pay.

Small groups

Never more than eight people on a tour.

Responsible travel

We use family-run stays and avoid single-use plastic.

Services offered

  • Guided tour packages
  • Airport transfers and private drivers
  • Hotel and homestay booking
  • Train, safari and boat reservations
  • Custom itineraries for families and groups
