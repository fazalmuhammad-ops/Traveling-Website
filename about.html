About Lanka Travellers

A small Colombo team that plans Sri Lanka trips the way we would plan them for our own families.

Our vision

To be the first name travellers think of for authentic, responsible travel in Sri Lanka.

Our mission

To design safe, well-paced tours that support local guides, family-run stays and communities.

Why choose Lanka Travellers?

Local guides

Licensed guides who grew up in the regions you visit.

Clear pricing

Entrance fees, transfers and breakfast are listed up front.

Flexible plans

Every package can be shortened, extended or combined.

24/7 support

A real person answers our phone during your whole trip.

Services we offer

  • Guided tour packages
  • Airport pickup and private drivers
  • Hotel and homestay booking
  • Train and safari arrangements
  • Custom itineraries
