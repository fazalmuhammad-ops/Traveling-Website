About Lanka Travellers

A passionate Colombo team that curates Sri Lankan journeys the way we would plan them for our own family.

🌟 Our Vision

To be the most trusted name travellers seek for authentic, immersive, and environmentally responsible travel across Sri Lanka.

🎯 Our Mission

To design safe, seamless, and well-paced tours that directly empower certified local guides, family-run boutique stays, and rural host communities.
